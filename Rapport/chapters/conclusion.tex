\chapter{Conclusion}

\section{AI tools}

Artificial intelligence tools, including ChatGPT and Claude, were used during the preparation of this thesis. Their use was limited to language correction, and translation into English. No artificial intelligence tool was used to generate or write the substantive content of the thesis. Such tools were, however, used to assist with LaTeX formatting and document layout.

With regard to the implementation, artificial intelligence tools were used for code correction and debugging, as well as for the creation of test files. The design, implementation, and validation of the algorithms and methods presented in this thesis remain the responsibility of the author.
